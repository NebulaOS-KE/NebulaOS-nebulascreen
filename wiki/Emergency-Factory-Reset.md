# Emergency factory reset

This page described the `S58factoryreset` service and the USB `emergency_factory_reset` trigger of
the old standalone screen installer. NebulaOS has neither (on NebulaOS, `S58` is the NebulaScreen
service).

For the reset options NebulaOS offers — reset the screen's settings, a clean NebulaOS reset, or a
switch to the other boot slot — see [Resetting](Resetting).
