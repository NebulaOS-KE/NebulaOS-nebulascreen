# Upgrading

This page described updating the old standalone screen installer on stock Creality firmware. That
flow does not exist for NebulaScreen: it ships inside the NebulaOS image and is updated with it.

See [Installation and updates](Installation#updates). Moving from a GuppyScreen-era NebulaOS image is
covered in [Installation](Installation#coming-from-a-guppyscreen-era-nebulaos-image).
